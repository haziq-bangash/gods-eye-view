What it does

One map to see every chokepoint.

Five live German freight layers, each explained on a plain card the moment you tap it.

Roads Rivers & weather Rail

Autobahn closures

Closures, warnings, exit closures and roadworks on the A1, A2, A3, A5, A7, A40 and A61, grouped by severity.

Truck parking

Rest-area spaces, marked red where a closure may shut a rest area or its access.

Rhine & Elbe levels

Gauges with Kaub, the shallowest point of the Middle Rhine, rated from "normal" to "shipping stopped".

Weather warnings

DWD areas in the warning colours, with what each one means for a truck.

Long-distance trains

ICE, IC, EC and night trains placed by timetable and held back by real delays.

How it works

From public data to a plain answer.

  1. 01

    Official sources

    Autobahn GmbH, PEGELONLINE, the German weather service, gtfs.de and Deutsche Bahn, all open or free to use.

  2. 02

    Bounded, cached routes

    Every request goes through the app's own server routes. The browser never chooses an upstream URL.

  3. 03

    A score you can read

    Closures, parking, water and weather combine into one corridor number, and every part is explained.

  4. 04

    Now, tonight or tomorrow

    One switch moves the whole map and the scores to the night or the next day.

Corridor risk score

One number per corridor. Four parts you can check.

Hamburg → Dortmund (A1), Hamburg → Ulm (A7) and the Rhine axis Duisburg → Karlsruhe, each scored from 0 to 100.

R = 0.35·C + 0.20·P + 0.25·H + 0.20·W
C

Closures 0.35

Autobahn events within 25 km of the corridor, weighted by severity on a saturating curve.

P

Truck parking 0.20

Usable spaces per 100 km. A rest area next to a closure doesn't count.

H

Hydrology 0.25

The Kaub water level, for river corridors. Low and high water both raise the score.

W

Weather 0.20

The highest DWD warning crossing the corridor now or within 12 hours.

Low · under 33Elevated · 33 to 66High · over 66

Weights and reference values are starting guesses, not calibrated against real delays. A reading aid, not a forecast. Parts with no data are left out and the rest rescaled.
